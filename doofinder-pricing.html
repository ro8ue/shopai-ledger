Doofinder pricing: what it really costs a Shopify store

Site search with recommendations and a quiz builder in every plan. Shopify prices are higher than Doofinder's general web prices, and plans are capped by monthly requests per product (search, recommendations, quiz).

How it's billed

Flat monthly plans capped by requests per month. A request is counted whenever Doofinder's server responds, and search-as-you-type counts from the third keystroke. Doofinder lists caps of 10k (Basic), 150k (Pro) and 400k (Advanced) requests per product.
