SB Helpdesk, Live Chat & FAQs pricing: what it really costs a Shopify store

Low-cost Shopify helpdesk priced by tickets, with OpenAI-integrated FAQs. It isn't an autonomous AI agent.

How AI usage is billed

There's no separate AI fee. AI is used for FAQs (OpenAI-integrated) and the plans are priced by ticket.

Assumptions: AI resolves 50% of conversations; monthly billing unless noted; taxes, add-ons, SMS/voice and phone fees excluded. How we calculate.

Free Shopify alternative: Shopify Inbox is free for chat. Helpcenter adds ticketing and FAQs cheaply. See Shopify Inbox.

Note: The annual prices are the yearly price divided by 12 ($400, $700 and $1,200 per year). Our estimates count every conversation as a ticket. On 26 Oct 2026 the listing dropped the $5.99 Basic plan and repriced the rest (was Standard $19.99/300 tickets, Advanced $49.99/1,000 tickets).
