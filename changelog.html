Price change log

Every pricing or plan change we spot, dated. We re-check every app's official pricing page weekly.

2026-10-19: Re:amaze: Starter plan confirmed without AI
  • Re:amaze support confirmed the flat $59 Starter plan doesn't include the AI Agent or any AI resolutions. AI resolutions come only with Basic (5/user), Pro (10/user) and Plus (20/user). Estimates were already based on Basic, so no cost figures changed.
2026-10-18: New category: AI search & product recommendation apps (11 apps)
  • Recorded official prices for Shopify Search & Discovery (free), Searchanise, Boost AI Search & Discovery, Fast Simon, Doofinder, Algolia, Wiser, LimeSpot, Rebuy, Klevu/Searchspring (Athos Commerce) and Nosto.
  • Noted: Boost moved new installs to GMV-based pricing in October 2025. Its pricing page ($239 Convert / $399 Accelerate) and help centre example ($299 Convert / $699 Accelerate) show different figures, so medium and large estimates are 'Not public / check site'.
  • Noted: Doofinder's Shopify App Store prices ($61/$186/$436) are higher than its general web prices ($49/$149/$349).
  • Noted: Rebuy, Klevu/Athos and Nosto don't publish volume-based prices; Fast Simon and Wiser publish prices only up to 30,000 sessions and 2,000 orders respectively.
2026-10-18: Tracker launched: baseline prices recorded for 13 AI support apps
  • Recorded official prices for Gorgias, Tidio (Lyro), Intercom Fin, Zendesk, HubSpot Service Hub, Shopify Inbox, Richpanel, Re:amaze, Freshdesk, Zipchat, Siena AI, Chatty and Helpcenter.
  • Noted: Zendesk's Shopify App Store listing still shows Suite Growth ($89/agent) and Suite Enterprise ($150/agent), which weren't on zendesk.com/pricing on this date.
  • Noted: Gorgias's pricing page markup contains two price sets. We use the detailed plan data (Basic $60, Pro $360, Advanced $900 monthly).
  • Noted: Intercom's Shopify listing shows monthly seat prices ($39/$99/$139) and intercom.com shows annual ($29/$85/$132).
