Aide pricing: what it really costs a Shopify store

Helpdesk with an AI agent, priced per AI reply with no seat fees.

Prices confirmedFree option overlapsPrice checked on 2026-11-07 from the official source.

Short answer: on our assumptions Aide comes to $39 a month for a small store, $39 for a medium store and $149 for a large store. The lowest priced paid support app we track for a medium store is HelpCenter: AI Helpdesk & Chat at $70. Put in your own volume with the free calculator, or see all support apps by real cost.

How AI usage is billed

Per AI reply, not per resolution and not per seat. Each plan includes a monthly AI reply allowance. On Free, AI pauses when the allowance is used and your team keeps answering. On paid plans, extra replies bill at the plan rate up to a monthly cap, then AI pauses.
