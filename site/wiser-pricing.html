Wiser pricing: what it really costs a Shopify store

AI product recommendations, frequently-bought-together, cart drawer and post-purchase upsells, priced by your total monthly store orders. AI search (IntelliSearch) is only on the Enterprise plan.

Partly confirmedFree option overlapsPrice checked 9 Nov 2026 from the official source.

Short answer: on our assumptions, Wiser costs $19 a month for a small store, $99 for a medium store and Not public / check site for a large store. It bills on orders. Compare it with the free Shopify Search & Discovery.

How it's billed

Flat monthly fee set automatically by your store's total orders that month: free to 50, $9 to 100, $19 to 300, $49 to 500, $99 to 1,000, $199 to 2,000. Above 2,000 orders is an Enterprise quote.
