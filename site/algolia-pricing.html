Algolia pricing: what it really costs a Shopify store

Developer-first search API used by large brands. The pay-as-you-go plans are very cheap at low volume, but you or a developer must build and maintain the Shopify integration.

Partly confirmedFree option overlapsPrice checked on 2026-11-08 from the official source.

Plans

PlanMonthly billingAnnual (per mo)What's included
Free$0—10K search requests/mo, 50K records, 5K recommendation requests

Grow Plus$0—$0 base; 10K requests incl., then $1.75/1K; AI ranking, AI synonyms, personalization
Elevate——Annual contract, NeuralSearch; custom pricing

How it's billed

Pay as you go. Grow Plus (keyword search plus AI ranking, AI synonyms and personalization) includes 10K search requests/month, then charges $1.75 per extra 1K. Plain keyword Grow is $0.50 per extra 1K. 100K records are included on both, then $0.40 per 1K. Search-as-you-type counts every keystroke as a request.

Estimated monthly cost by store size

Small store (500 products, 200 orders, $15,000 revenue, 10,000 sessions, 15,000 search requests a month)
$8.75/mo
Grow Plus (pay as you go)
  • Search requests: 15,000 − 10,000 included = 5,000 extra
  • 5K × $1.75 per 1K = $8.75/mo (Grow Plus, with AI ranking/synonyms)
  • Records: 500 products fit within the 100,000 included records (variants/replicas can add more)
  • Keyword-only Grow would be $2.50/mo. Developer/integration time not included.
Medium store (5,000 products, 1,000 orders, $75,000 revenue, 40,000 sessions, 60,000 search requests a month)
$87.50/mo
Grow Plus (pay as you go)
  • Search requests: 60,000 − 10,000 included = 50,000 extra
  • 50K × $1.75 per 1K = $87.50/mo (Grow Plus, with AI ranking/synonyms)
  • Records: 5,000 products fit within the 100,000 included records (variants/replicas can add more)
  • Keyword-only Grow would be $25/mo. Developer/integration time not included.
Large store (25,000 products, 4,000 orders, $300,000 revenue, 150,000 sessions, 225,000 search requests a month)
$376.25/mo
Grow Plus (pay as you go)
  • Search requests: 225,000 − 10,000 included = 215,000 extra
  • 215K × $1.75 per 1K = $376.25/mo (Grow Plus, with AI ranking/synonyms)
  • Records: 25,000 products fit within the 100,000 included records (variants/replicas can add more)
  • Keyword-only Grow would be $107.50/mo. Developer/integration time not included.

Monthly billing unless noted; taxes, add-ons, setup/developer work and negotiated discounts excluded. How we calculate.
