Price moves

Price moves: what changed, and when

Real price changes and vendor replies only, each with a date and a source. Weeks where nothing changed are on the full change log.

Price change · 2026-10-26

Helpcenter (SB Helpdesk)
  • SB Helpdesk (Helpcenter) repriced on the Shopify App Store. The $5.99 Basic plan is gone. Standard went from $19.99 (300 tickets) to $40 (500 tickets, then $0.15/ticket). Advanced went from $49.99 to $70 (1,000 tickets, overage up from $0.10 to $0.12). A new Enterprise plan is $120 for 2,000 tickets, then $0.10/ticket.
  • Helpcenter estimates: small store $19.99 to $40, medium $49.99 to $70, large $349.99 to $320 (the new Enterprise plan is cheaper at 4,000 tickets than the old Advanced plan plus overage).

Source: Shopify App Store listing

Price change · 2026-10-26

Fast Simon
  • Fast Simon raised its Essential plan (10,000 monthly sessions) from $99.99 to $114.99 a month ($999.96 to $1,149.96 a year). Starter ($39.99) and Top Pro ($299.99) didn't change. Our small-store estimate moves from $99.99 to $114.99.

Source: Shopify App Store listing

Vendor reply · 2026-10-19

LimeSpot
  • LimeSpot support (Shihab) pointed us to https://limespot.com/shopify for Turbo and Max pricing. We read the order and revenue sliders from that page's published tiers.
  • Turbo above 250 orders is now public: medium store (1,000 orders) is Turbo $149.99/mo instead of Max $225/mo. Large store (4,000 orders / $300k revenue) stays on Max at $525/mo because Turbo would be $649.99.
  • Estimates now pick the cheaper of Turbo (by orders) and Max (by revenue) for each store size. List prices still ignore the 50% lifetime new-signup offer.

Source: LimeSpot Shopify pricing page

Vendor reply · 2026-10-19

Re:amaze
  • Re:amaze support confirmed the flat $59 Starter plan doesn't include the AI Agent or any AI resolutions. AI resolutions come only with Basic (5/user), Pro (10/user) and Plus (20/user). Estimates were already based on Basic, so no cost figures changed.

Source: Re:amaze pricing page
