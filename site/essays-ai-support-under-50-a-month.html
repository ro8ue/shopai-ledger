AI support under $50 a month?

Price the support app at the size you will be in a year, not the size you are today.

At 200 chats a month nothing paid with a real AI agent comes in under $50. At 1,000 chats most bills grow 3x to 5x, and the ranking reshuffles.

Essays / AI support under $50 a month?

The question

A store owner on Reddit asked for the cheapest AI chatbot that actually works for a small Shopify store, on a budget under $50 a month. Our small store gets 200 support conversations a month, has one person answering, and the AI handles half. Here is the cheapest plan that covers it, from official pricing pages checked 26 Oct 2026.

Under $50, only the free one fits

Shopify Inbox: $0. Free, with an AI sales associate trained on your catalog, policies and store content.

Helpcenter: $40. Cheap, but it gives you AI-written FAQs, not an AI agent.

Chatty: $59.99. Basic is $19.99 for 100 AI conversations. The other 100 cost $0.40 each.

Tidio: $68. Lyro costs $39 for up to 200 conversations, plus Tidio's $29 plan for the chats a person answers.

Zipchat: $80. Starter gives you 750 AI replies, which Zipchat says is about 300 conversations.

After that come the full helpdesks in a tight $109.75 to $138 band: Re:amaze, Freshdesk, HubSpot, Gorgias and Intercom Fin. Start with Shopify Inbox, and pay for an AI agent only when you need one inbox for email, social and returns.

What 5x the chats does to each bill

Move the same store to 1,000 conversations a month and two seats. Gorgias goes from $130 to $670 (5.2x), Re:amaze from $109.75 to $474.50 (4.3x), Zendesk from $259 to $1,118 (4.3x), HubSpot from $120 to $420 (3.5x) and Chatty from $59.99 to $199 (3.3x).

Two apps barely move. Richpanel goes from $319 to $478 (1.5x), because a small store already pays its $200 AI minimum, so it climbs from 10th to 5th among paid AI apps. Siena AI goes from $840 to $1,200 (1.4x), because most of its bill is the $750 platform fee.

The one that is cheapest now may not be cheapest then.

What counts as "one"

Chatty bills per AI conversation. Tidio counts a conversation once Lyro sends a reply, whether or not it solves anything. Zipchat counts every AI reply, and a back and forth uses about 2.5 of them. Re:amaze, HubSpot and Zendesk bill only when the AI resolves the conversation. Two stores with the same number of chats can get very different bills.
