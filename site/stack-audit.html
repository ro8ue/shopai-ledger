The usual way

The usual way to choose an app is to compare starting prices, read a recommendation, and add another tool. That misses what you already have, what each app counts as usage, and which features you actually need.

The Stillprice way

The Stillprice way starts with your current stack. We apply the public price ledger to your volumes, look for overlapping jobs, then write a practical order of action. Sometimes that means a cheaper plan. Sometimes it means removing an app. Sometimes the right answer is to leave a working setup alone.

We do not promise savings. We show the math and explain the tradeoffs.

Before you decide

Frequently asked questions

How much does it cost?

The Stack Audit is priced at $149 USD, one time. We are waiving that fee for these first requests. There is no checkout and no card to enter. If we start charging later, that would apply to new requests, not to a report already requested while the fee is waived.

What exactly do I get?

An emailed written report of about two pages covering your current support and search apps, public pricing at your volume, what to cut, what to keep, a simpler stack where appropriate, and a sequenced action plan. Every estimated cost change has assumptions. If keeping your current setup is the sensible answer, we say so.

How long does it take?

48 hours from receipt of your complete stack details. That means elapsed hours, not business days. We ask for any missing essentials before confirming the delivery deadline. No call is required.

What do I send you?

Your support and search app names, plans if you know them, approximate monthly tickets or chats, product count, spend if known, main complaint and requirements such as needing an email helpdesk or having no developer. We may ask for the billing meter a particular app uses. Do not send passwords or customer records. Redacted billing screenshots are optional.

Can I just use the free calculator or ChatGPT?

Yes, if you want to do the analysis yourself. The free calculator is useful for support cost estimates, and ChatGPT can help you organize questions. This service adds a review of your actual support and search stack against the ledger, the plan limits and the jobs you need done. The $149 price is for that applied analysis and written decisions, not access to secret prices or a proprietary score. The fee being waived does not change what you receive.

Will you install apps or cancel them for me?

No. Installation, cancellations, migration and ongoing management are not included. The plan identifies what to check before making a change. It does not certify that a replacement has every feature you use.

What if my stack is already sensible?

Then the report says what to keep and why. The review is the assessment, not a promised saving or a requirement to switch apps.

Do you earn money from app recommendations?

Stillprice is an affiliate of Gorgias and Searchanise. We may earn a commission if you sign up through a link labeled affiliate link, at no extra cost to you. That relationship is a conflict when we recommend either app, so we disclose it here and in your report. Affiliate status never changes the prices, calculations, cost ordering or verdict. We consider free and nonaffiliate options where relevant, and recommend cutting or keeping an affiliate app on the same basis as any other.
