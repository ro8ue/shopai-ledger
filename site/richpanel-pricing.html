Richpanel pricing: what it really costs a Shopify store

Ecommerce helpdesk sold per seat, with AI billed on a token meter rather than per resolution.

Partly confirmedPrice checked on 2026-11-09 from the official source.

Short answer: on our assumptions Richpanel comes to $319 a month for a small store, $478 for a medium store and $1,555 for a large store. The lowest priced paid support app we track for a medium store is Aide at $39. Put in your own volume with the free calculator, or see all support apps by real cost.

How AI usage is billed

Token-based credits: $0.60/credit monthly or $0.50 annual. Richpanel says about $0.20 covers a whole AI-handled conversation at the annual rate. AI minimum is $200/mo.

Estimated monthly cost by store size

Small store (200 conversations, 1 seat, 100 AI-resolved)
$319/mo
1 helpdesk seat + AI meter
  • Seats: 1 × $119 (monthly) = $119
  • AI: 200 AI-handled conversations × ~$0.24 = $48; minimum $200 → $200
  • Total ≈ $319/mo (approximate: AI cost depends on tokens).
Medium store (1,000 conversations, 2 seats, 500 AI-resolved)
$478/mo
2 helpdesk seats + AI meter
  • Seats: 2 × $119 (monthly) = $238
  • AI: 1,000 AI-handled conversations × ~$0.24 = $240; minimum $200 → $240
  • Total ≈ $478/mo (approximate: AI cost depends on tokens).
Large store (4,000 conversations, 5 seats, 2,000 AI-resolved)
$1,555/mo
5 helpdesk seats + AI meter
  • Seats: 5 × $119 (monthly) = $595
  • AI: 4,000 AI-handled conversations × ~$0.24 = $960; minimum $200 → $960
  • Total ≈ $1,555/mo (approximate: AI cost depends on tokens).

Assumptions: AI resolves 50% of conversations; monthly billing unless noted; taxes, add-ons, SMS/voice and phone fees excluded. How we calculate.
