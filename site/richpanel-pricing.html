Richpanel pricing: what it really costs a Shopify store

Ecommerce helpdesk sold per seat, with AI billed on a token meter rather than per resolution.

Partly confirmedPrice checked 15 Nov 2026 on Richpanel's official pricing page.

Short answer: on our assumptions, Richpanel costs $289 a month for a small store, $378 for a medium store and $1,245 for a large store. For a medium store, the lowest priced paid support app we track is Aide at $39. Run your own volume through the free calculator, or see all support apps by real cost.

How AI usage is billed

Customer Service AI is billed at $0.20 per AI-handled conversation. The official pricing page's calculator script still applies a $200/mo minimum. Helpdesk seats are priced separately; we use Pro, the cheapest published seat plan, at $89/mo ($69/mo billed annually).
