Zipchat pricing: what it really costs a Shopify store

AI agent for ecommerce chat, email and WhatsApp, priced by AI replies per month. It's an AI layer, not a full helpdesk.

Partly confirmedFree option overlapsPrice checked on 2026-10-26 from the official source.

Short answer: on our assumptions Zipchat comes to $80 a month for a small store, $300 for a medium store and $960 for a large store. The lowest priced paid support app we track for a medium store is Aide at $39. Put in your own volume with the free calculator, or see all support apps by real cost.

How AI usage is billed

Per AI reply: every outbound AI message counts. Plans include a reply allowance, and extra packs cost $49 for 250 replies ($49 for 500 on Scale Plus).
