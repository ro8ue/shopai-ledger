Tidio pricing: what it really costs a Shopify store

Live chat and helpdesk with Lyro, an AI agent sold as its own plan. Human and AI conversations are billed separately.

Partly confirmedFree option overlapsPrice checked 9 Nov 2026 from the official source.

Short answer: on our assumptions, Tidio costs $68 a month for a small store, a quote (price not public) for a medium store and a quote (price not public) for a large store. How AI usage is billed

Per Lyro conversation: any conversation with at least one Lyro reply counts once, whether or not it's resolved. Lyro plan: $39/mo for up to 200 Lyro conversations. Every account gets 50 Lyro conversations once.
