How it's billed

Each package (Cart & Merchandising, Checkout & Post-Purchase, Search & Collections, Flows & A/B Testing) is priced by orders per month. The public table starts at $25/mo for Checkout & Post-Purchase and $35/mo for Search & Collections, both at up to 500 orders. Bundling saves 10% on two packages, 20% on three and 30% on four. Rebuy bills on a 30-day cycle based on the previous 30 days' orders.
