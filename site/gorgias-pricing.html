Gorgias pricing: what it really costs a Shopify store

An ecommerce helpdesk billed by ticket volume, not seats. Every plan includes an AI Agent that bills per use.

Prices confirmedFree option overlapsPrice checked 9 Nov 2026 from the official source.

Our verdict

Honest take: Gorgias

Who it suits. Stores that live in email and social tickets and want orders, returns and refunds handled in one helpdesk. Gorgias bills by tickets, not seats, so a big team on modest volume does fine.

Who overpays, and why. Small and medium stores that mostly need chat. On our numbers a medium store pays about $670 a month on Pro. Chatty costs $199 and Zipchat $300 at the same volume. Nearly half the Gorgias bill, $310, is AI: 500 AI interactions, minus the 190 included, at $1.00 each.

Gotchas.

  • Every AI interaction also counts as a helpdesk ticket, so automation eats into your ticket allowance too.
  • The pricing page lists $1.50 per interaction above a plan's included allowance.
  • The code behind that page carries a second, higher price set (Basic $77, Pro $471, Advanced $1,227). Get your quote in writing.
  • Advanced ($900 a month) is sold through a demo. You cannot just click to buy it.

Bottom line. Good product, often poor value at or below about 1,000 conversations a month. We earn a commission if you sign up through our link. That does not change this verdict.

Opinion, written by a person. Numbers come from the estimates on this site. Affiliate status never changes a verdict.

Short answer: on our assumptions, Gorgias costs $130 a month for a small store, $670 for a medium store and $2,370 for a large store. How AI usage is billed

Each automated interaction costs $1.00 on a monthly contract or $0.90 on annual. It also counts as a helpdesk ticket. Gorgias's page also lists $1.50 per interaction for overage above a plan's included allowance.
