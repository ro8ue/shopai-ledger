Gorgias pricing: what it really costs a Shopify store

Ecommerce helpdesk priced by ticket volume (not seats), with a usage-priced AI Agent on every plan.

Prices confirmedFree option overlapsPrice checked on 2026-10-26 from the official source.

Our verdict

Honest take: Gorgias

Who it suits. Stores that live in email and social tickets every day and want orders, returns and refunds handled inside one helpdesk. It is priced by tickets, not seats, so a big team on modest volume does fine.

Who overpays, and why. Small and medium stores that mostly need chat. On our numbers a medium store pays about $670 a month on Pro, against $199 for Chatty or $300 for Zipchat at the same volume. Nearly half of that, $310, is the AI line: 500 AI interactions minus the 190 included, at $1.00 each.

Gotchas.

  • Every AI interaction also counts as a helpdesk ticket, so automation eats your ticket allowance too.
  • The pricing page lists $1.50 per interaction above a plan's included allowance, and its markup carries a second, higher price set (Basic $77, Pro $471, Advanced $1,227). Get your quote in writing.
  • Advanced ($900 a month) is sold through a demo, so you cannot just click to buy it.

Bottom line. Good product, often poor value below about 1,000 conversations a month. We earn a commission if you sign up through our link. That does not change this verdict.

Opinion, written by a person. Numbers come from the estimates on this site. Affiliate status never changes a verdict.

Short answer: on our assumptions Gorgias comes to $130 a month for a small store, $670 for a medium store and $2,370 for a large store. How AI usage is billed

Per automated interaction: $1.00 on monthly contracts, $0.90 on annual. Each AI interaction also counts as a helpdesk ticket. Gorgias's page also lists $1.50 per interaction for overage above a plan's included allowance.
