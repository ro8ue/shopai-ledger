Chatty AI Chatbot & Live Chat pricing: what it really costs a Shopify store

Shopify-native AI chatbot and live chat priced by AI conversations, with unlimited human conversations.

Short answer: on our assumptions Chatty AI Chatbot & Live Chat comes to $59.99 a month for a small store, $199 for a medium store and $1,399 for a large store.
