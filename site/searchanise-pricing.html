Searchanise pricing: what it really costs a Shopify store

A search and filter app billed only by how many products are in your catalog. Paid plans share the same core search features, including AI personalization, filters and recommendations. Bigger plans add support hours and a dedicated server.

Our verdict

Honest take: Searchanise

Who it suits. Most small and medium catalogs. You pay by product count only, and searches are unlimited, so a traffic spike or Black Friday does not raise the bill. On our numbers that is $19 for a small store, $39 for a medium store and $139 for a large one.

Who doesn't need to pay. Stores with up to 25 products, which fit the free plan. Also stores that only need filters, synonyms and boosts, which Shopify Search & Discovery does for $0.

Gotchas.

  • Tiers jump with catalog size: at 7,501 products you move from $39 to $89.
  • Bigger plans add support hours and a dedicated server, not better core search. Do not upgrade for features.
  • Annual billing saves up to 17%. The monthly figures on this page are the higher ones.

Bottom line. Fair value and the most predictable paid bill in the search category. We earn a commission if you sign up through our link. That does not change this verdict.

How it's billed

No per-search or per-session fees. You pay a flat monthly price set by how many active, searchable products you have. The tiers: free up to 25 products, then 1,500, 7,500, 15,000, 30,000, 50,000 and 300,000. Personalized AI search is included on every paid plan.
