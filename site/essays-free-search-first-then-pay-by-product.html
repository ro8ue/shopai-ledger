Free search first, then pay by product count

The spread in Shopify AI search bills is not about AI. It is about what each app counts.

Six search apps, three sample stores. A large store pays $0, $139, $376.25 or $436 a month depending on whether the app meters products, search requests or sessions.

Essays / Free search first, then pay by product count

The question

A store owner on r/ecommerce asked something we hear a lot: native Shopify search misses typos, but every search app "scales up way too fast based on catalog size or clicks." So is the free app enough, and if not, which bill grows slowest?

From the official pricing pages, checked 26 Oct 2026 on monthly billing, a large store pays $0 for Shopify Search & Discovery, $139 for Searchanise, $376.25 for Algolia and $436 for Doofinder. Boost and Fast Simon stop publishing prices above the small store.

Start with the free one

Shopify Search & Discovery is $0 at every size. It gives you synonyms, product boosts and filters. Pull your zero result searches, clean up titles and tags, add synonyms, then decide. If you skip that step, a paid app just indexes the same messy catalog.

Four apps, four meters

Searchanise counts products. Unlimited searches. $19 small (500 products), $39 medium (5,000), $139 large (25,000).

Algolia counts search requests, and search as you type sends one per keystroke. $8.75 small, $87.50 medium, $376.25 large, before any developer time.

Doofinder counts search requests too, in plan steps. $186 for both small and medium on Pro, then $436 on Advanced.

Fast Simon counts all store sessions. Essential is $114.99 for the small store (up from $99.99 in October). Above 30,000 sessions there is no public price.

So a busy Black Friday does nothing to a Searchanise bill, and quite a lot to a per request bill. That is the "scales too fast" feeling, and it depends on your meter, not your catalog quality.

Where prices go dark

Boost starts at $29 for the small store. Its public pages put Convert at about $239 to $299 for our medium store, but the exact sub tier is not public, so we do not put a number on it. Fast Simon is the same above 30,000 sessions. Klevu and Nosto publish no volume price at all. If a vendor makes you ask, we mark it not public rather than guess.

The verdict

Small store: stay on free Search & Discovery until you have proof search is losing sales. If you need typo tolerance, Searchanise at $19 or Algolia at $8.75 are the cheapest paths, but Algolia needs someone technical.

Medium store: Searchanise at $39 is the lowest published price for a no code app. Algolia at $87.50 makes sense only if you have a developer.

Large store: Searchanise at $139 is still the lowest published bill. Algolia and Doofinder cost roughly three times that on our numbers, and they buy you more control, not a cheaper bill.

One caveat: our request count assumes about 1.5 search requests per session. Your theme and traffic can move that a lot, so check the pages below with your own numbers before you commit.
