Zendesk pricing: what it really costs a Shopify store

Seat-based helpdesk. Every plan includes AI agents with a small allowance of automated resolutions, and usage beyond it is billed per resolution.

Prices confirmedPrice checked 9 Nov 2026 on Zendesk's official pricing page.

Short answer: on our assumptions, Zendesk costs $259 a month for a small store, $1,118 for a medium store and $4,295 for a large store. For a medium store, the lowest priced paid support app we track is Aide at $39. Run your own volume through the free calculator, or see all support apps by real cost.

How AI usage is billed

Per automated resolution. Each plan includes 5 (Team plans) or 10 (Suite Professional) per agent per month. Beyond that, $1.50 each committed or $2.00 pay-as-you-go.

Estimated monthly cost by store size

Suite Team × 1 + pay-as-you-go resolutions

  • Automated resolutions: 100 − 5 included = 95 × $2 = $190
  • Total $259/mo. With committed resolutions at $1.50: $211.50/mo.

  • Seats: 2 × $69 (monthly) = $138
  • Automated resolutions: 500 − 10 included = 490 × $2 = $980
  • Total $1,118/mo. With committed resolutions at $1.50: $873/mo.
Large store (4,000 conversations, 5 seats, 2,000 AI-resolved)
$4,295/mo
Suite Team × 5 + pay-as-you-go resolutions
  • Seats: 5 × $69 (monthly) = $345
  • Automated resolutions: 2,000 − 25 included = 1,975 × $2 = $3,950
  • Total $4,295/mo. With committed resolutions at $1.50: $3,307.50/mo.

Assumptions: AI resolves 50% of conversations. Monthly billing unless noted. Excludes taxes, add-ons, SMS/voice and phone fees. How we calculate.
