HubSpot Service Hub pricing: what it really costs a Shopify store

CRM-connected helpdesk. Its AI Customer Agent needs the Professional plan or higher and runs on HubSpot Credits.

Prices confirmedPrice checked 26 Oct 2026 on HubSpot Service Hub's official pricing page.

Short answer: on our assumptions, HubSpot Service Hub costs $120 a month for a small store, $420 for a medium store and $1,470 for a large store. For a medium store, the lowest priced paid support app we track is Aide at $39. Run your own volume through the free calculator, or see all support apps by real cost.

How AI usage is billed

$0.50 per resolved conversation (50 HubSpot Credits). A resolution means the conversation wasn't handed to a human for 72 hours. Professional includes 3,000 credits/mo (about 60 resolutions if nothing else uses them).
