HelpCenter: AI Helpdesk & Chat pricing: what it really costs a Shopify store

Low-cost Shopify helpdesk priced by tickets, with OpenAI-integrated FAQs. It isn't an autonomous AI agent.

Prices confirmedFree option overlapsPrice checked 9 Nov 2026 on HelpCenter: AI Helpdesk & Chat's official pricing page.

Short answer: on our assumptions, HelpCenter: AI Helpdesk & Chat costs $40 a month for a small store, $70 for a medium store and $320 for a large store. For a medium store, the lowest priced paid support app we track is Aide at $39. Run your own volume through the free calculator, or see all support apps by real cost.
