Gorgias alternatives, compared on real cost

On our assumptions, Gorgias costs about $195 a month for a small store, $1,015 for a medium store and $3,635 for a large one (see the math). Here is how the alternatives compare for the same store.

Our verdict

Honest take: Gorgias alternatives

Who it suits. If you need a real helpdesk, Re:amaze, HubSpot and Intercom Fin land between $420 and $573 a month for our medium store, below Gorgias at $1,015. If you only need chat with AI, Chatty ($199) and Zipchat ($300) cost far less.

Who overpays, and why. Anyone paying for a full helpdesk when Shopify Inbox would do. Inbox is free and now has an AI agent. On our numbers Siena AI is the priciest at medium ($1,200); Zendesk is about $1,118 medium and $4,295 for a large one.

Gotchas.

  • Seat based tools (Intercom, Zendesk, HubSpot, Re:amaze, Freshdesk, Richpanel) get expensive fast as your team grows.
  • Chat only tools (Zipchat, Chatty, Shopify Inbox) do not handle email tickets, so they are not a like for like swap.
  • Tidio's prices above the small store tier are not public, so we cannot rank it there.

Bottom line. Price the job you actually have. Most small stores are buying more helpdesk than they use.

Opinion, written by a person. Numbers come from the estimates on this site. Affiliate status never changes a verdict.
