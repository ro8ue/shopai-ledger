Fast Simon pricing: what it really costs a Shopify store

AI search and collection merchandising app priced by monthly sessions, meaning every store visit, not just visits that use search. Paid plans support up to 40,000 products.

How it's billed

Flat monthly plans capped by monthly sessions: 100 (free), 2,000, 10,000 and 30,000. Above 30,000 sessions you need a custom/Enterprise quote. AI multimodal search is on Top Pro.
