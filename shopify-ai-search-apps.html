What Shopify AI search & recommendation apps really cost

Search and recommendation apps bill on completely different things: product count, store visits, keystrokes, orders or revenue. We converted each official price list into one monthly bill for the same three stores.

Small store: 500 products, 200 orders/mo, $15,000 revenue/mo, 10,000 sessions/mo, 15,000 search requests/moMedium store: 5,000 products, 1,000 orders/mo, $75,000 revenue/mo, 40,000 sessions/mo, 60,000 search requests/moLarge store: 25,000 products, 4,000 orders/mo, $300,000 revenue/mo, 150,000 sessions/mo, 225,000 search requests/mo

Prices checked 2026-10-19 from official pricing pages and Shopify App Store listings. Monthly billing unless noted. Sorted by medium-store cost. "Not public / check site" means the vendor doesn't publish a price for that size, so we don't guess.

What each app bills on

Product count

Searchanise: flat price by catalog size, unlimited searches.

Sessions or search requests

Fast Simon counts store sessions. Doofinder and Algolia count search requests, which with search-as-you-type means keystrokes, so traffic spikes can push you up a plan.

Orders or revenue

Wiser, LimeSpot Turbo and Rebuy price by monthly orders. Boost (GMV) and LimeSpot Max price by revenue, so the bill grows as you sell more.

Quote only

Klevu/Searchspring (Athos Commerce) and Nosto don't publish prices and sell annual plans.

Not all of these do the same job: Wiser, LimeSpot and Rebuy are mainly recommendation/upsell tools, while Searchanise, Boost, Fast Simon, Doofinder and Algolia are mainly search & filter tools. How we calculate.
