Before you decide

Questions

Can I just use the free calculator or ChatGPT?

The public price pages and the free calculator show published prices. The audit applies those numbers to your actual apps, volume, and overlap, then writes the cut list. You can also explore search app prices for free.

Do I need to get on a call?

No. You send written answers. The report comes by email.

How quickly is the report ready?

Within 48 hours of receiving your complete stack details. That means elapsed hours, not business days.

Why not just add the app a thread recommended?

Because overlap and public pricing at your volume are what decide whether that app is worth adding.
