Bottom line on cost

  • Small store: Searchanise is lower on our assumptions ($19 vs $29, a $10/mo difference).
  • Medium store: can't compare exactly, because one price isn't public at this size (Searchanise: $39, Essential; Boost: Not public / check site, Convert ($239–$299 per official pages)).
  • Large store: can't compare exactly, because one price isn't public at this size (Searchanise: $139, Growth; Boost: Not public / check site, Convert/Accelerate (sub-tier not public)).

Cost is only one factor. Features, merchandising controls, AI quality and integrations differ, and your real catalog, traffic and orders will move these numbers. See the full math on each app's page.
