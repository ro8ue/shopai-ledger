Why the bill surprises people

Per-resolution billing

Gorgias, Zendesk, Intercom Fin, HubSpot, Re:amaze and Siena charge for each conversation the AI closes. Better automation means a bigger AI line on the bill.

Per-conversation or per-reply billing

Tidio Lyro, Chatty, Richpanel, Freshdesk and Zipchat count conversations (or messages) the AI touches, resolved or not.

Seats still matter

Intercom, Zendesk, HubSpot, Re:amaze, Freshdesk and Richpanel also charge per human agent seat, and HubSpot Professional adds a one-time onboarding fee.
