Freshdesk pricing: what it really costs a Shopify store

Seat-based helpdesk. The Freddy AI Agent is billed in session packs after a one-time free allowance.

Partly confirmedPrice checked on 2026-10-26 from the official source.

Plans

PlanMonthly billingAnnual (per mo)What's included
Growth—$19Per agent. Freddy AI Agent
Pro—$55Per agent. Freddy Copilot available at $29/agent/mo
Enterprise—$89Per agent

How AI usage is billed

$49 per 100 AI Agent sessions. A session is one interaction with the AI agent (for email, a 72-hour window). The first 500 sessions are free, once per account.

Estimated monthly cost by store size

Small store (200 conversations, 1 seat, 100 AI-resolved)
$117/mo
Growth × 1 (annual) + session packs
  • Seats: 1 × $19 (annual billing) = $19
  • AI sessions: 200 AI-handled conversations → 2 packs × $49 = $98
  • Total $117/mo
Medium store (1,000 conversations, 2 seats, 500 AI-resolved)
$528/mo
Growth × 2 (annual) + session packs
  • Seats: 2 × $19 (annual billing) = $38
  • AI sessions: 1,000 AI-handled conversations → 10 packs × $49 = $490
  • Total $528/mo
Large store (4,000 conversations, 5 seats, 2,000 AI-resolved)
$2,055/mo
Growth × 5 (annual) + session packs
  • Seats: 5 × $19 (annual billing) = $95
  • AI sessions: 4,000 AI-handled conversations → 40 packs × $49 = $1,960
  • Total $2,055/mo

Assumptions: AI resolves 50% of conversations; monthly billing unless noted; taxes, add-ons, SMS/voice and phone fees excluded. How we calculate.

Not confirmed / not public:
  • Monthly-billing seat prices (we captured annual-billing prices only)
Check the vendor's site for these.

Note: Our estimates use the annual-billing seat price, so monthly billing will cost more. The one-time 500 free sessions are left out.
