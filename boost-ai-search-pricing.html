Boost pricing: what it really costs a Shopify store

One of the biggest Shopify search & filter apps. Since October 2025 new installs are priced by your store's monthly GMV (gross sales), not product count, across three packages: Launch, Convert and Accelerate.

How it's billed

Monthly subscription set by your 'Base GMV' (average gross sales over the last 3 months), plus an overage of $2 per $1,000 of GMV above your plan's limit (capped). AI search, synonyms and semantic search are included; AI personalization is on Accelerate.
