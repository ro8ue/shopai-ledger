LimeSpot pricing: what it really costs a Shopify store

AI recommendations, bundles and upsells. Turbo is priced by orders and isn't available on Shopify Plus. Max (segmentation, A/B tests, email/SMS personalization) is priced by Online Store revenue.

Prices confirmedFree option overlapsPrice checked on 2026-10-26 from the official source.

How it's billed

Turbo is priced by Online Store orders/30 days (not for Shopify Plus): free to 10, $9.99 to 100, $19.99 to 250, $34.99 to 350, $49.99 to 500, $124.99 to 750, $149.99 to 1,000, $249.99 to 1,500, $299.99 to 2,000, $499.99 to 3,000, $649.99 to 4,000, $799.99 to 5,000, $1,249.99 to 7,500, $1,749.99 to 10,000, $2,499.99 above 10,000. Max is priced by Online Store revenue/30 days: $50 up to $15K, $75 to $25K, $150 to $50K, $225 to $75K, $300 to $100K, $375 to $200K, $525 to $400K, and so on up to $3,200 for $5M.
