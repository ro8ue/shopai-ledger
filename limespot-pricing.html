AI recommendations, bundles and upsells. Turbo is priced by orders and isn't available on Shopify Plus. Max (segmentation, A/B tests, email/SMS personalization) is priced by Online Store revenue.

How it's billed

Turbo starts at $9.99/mo and rises with orders (LimeSpot's example: up to 250 orders = $19.99); it's free for up to 10 orders. Max is priced by Online Store revenue per 30 days: $50 up to $15K, $75 up to $25K, $150 up to $50K, $225 up to $75K, $300 up to $100K, $375 up to $200K, $525 up to $400K, and so on up to $3,200 for $5M.
