HubSpot Service Hub pricing: what it really costs a Shopify store

CRM-connected helpdesk. Its AI Customer Agent needs the Professional plan or higher and runs on HubSpot Credits.

Prices confirmedPrice checked on 2026-10-18 from the official source.

How AI usage is billed

$0.50 per resolved conversation (50 HubSpot Credits). A resolution means the conversation wasn't handed to a human for 72 hours. Professional includes 3,000 credits/mo (about 60 resolutions if nothing else uses them).

Estimated monthly cost by store size

Small store (200 conversations, 1 seat, 100 AI-resolved)
$120/mo
Professional × 1 seat
  • Seats: 1 × $100 (monthly) = $100
  • Resolutions: 100 − ~60 covered by 3,000 included credits = 40 × $0.50 = $20
  • Total $120/mo, plus $1,500 one-time onboarding.
Medium store (1,000 conversations, 2 seats, 500 AI-resolved)
$420/mo
Professional × 2 seats
  • Seats: 2 × $100 (monthly) = $200
  • Resolutions: 500 − ~60 covered by 3,000 included credits = 440 × $0.50 = $220
  • Total $420/mo, plus $1,500 one-time onboarding.
Large store (4,000 conversations, 5 seats, 2,000 AI-resolved)
$1,470/mo
Professional × 5 seats
  • Seats: 5 × $100 (monthly) = $500
  • Resolutions: 2,000 − ~60 covered by 3,000 included credits = 1,940 × $0.50 = $970
  • Total $1,470/mo, plus $1,500 one-time onboarding.

Assumptions: AI resolves 50% of conversations; monthly billing unless noted; taxes, add-ons, SMS/voice and phone fees excluded. How we calculate.
