Affiliate disclosure

Right now, ShopAI Ledger has no affiliate links. Every "official pricing" link goes straight to the vendor.

In future we may join affiliate programs run by some app makers. If we do, those links will be labelled, and this page will list which apps pay us. Affiliate status never changes the prices we show, the math, or the sort order (always by estimated cost).

We may also offer clearly-labelled sponsored placements. Sponsored content is always marked "Sponsored" and never changes an app's price data. See Advertise.

ShopAI Ledger is independent and not affiliated with Shopify or any app listed. Prices are in USD, checked by hand on the date shown, and can change at any time. Always confirm on the vendor's site.
