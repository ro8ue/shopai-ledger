How it's billed

Each package (Cart & Merchandising, Checkout & Post Purchase, Search & Collections, Flows & A/B Testing) is priced by orders per month, from $25/mo for one package. Bundling saves 10% on two, 20% on three and 30% on four. Premium support is $499/mo. Rebuy bills on a 30-day cycle based on the previous 30 days' orders.
