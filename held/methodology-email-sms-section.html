Email and SMS apps: store sizes

Small store: 2,500 contacts, 25,000 emails/mo, 500 SMS subscribers, 3,000 US SMS/moMedium store: 15,000 contacts, 150,000 emails/mo, 3,000 SMS subscribers, 18,000 US SMS/moLarge store: 75,000 contacts, 750,000 emails/mo, 15,000 SMS subscribers, 90,000 US SMS/mo

Email and SMS apps count two things: contacts (or active profiles) and sends. The three sizes use the same order and revenue anchors as our search and recommendation stores (200 / 1,000 / 4,000 orders a month, $15,000 / $75,000 / $300,000 a month). The contact, email and SMS numbers are our assumptions, not vendor figures.

  • About 10 emails per contact per month, across campaigns and automations. That fits inside Klaviyo's included send capacity (10 emails per profile) and under Omnisend Standard's credit cap (12 emails per contact).
  • SMS subscribers are about 20% of the contact list. Each one gets about 6 US texts per month, single-segment SMS, no MMS.
  • We use monthly list prices in USD and pick the cheapest published plan that covers the contact and send assumptions. We leave out taxes, optional short codes, optional AI add-ons and negotiated discounts. Where a vendor publishes average US carrier fees separately, we add them.
  • Postscript is priced as SMS only. It does not publish email rates (checked 11 Nov 2026), so we show email as "not published" and do not estimate it.
