Re:amaze pricing: what it really costs a Shopify store

Helpdesk billed per team member, with a small AI-resolution allowance per user.

Plans

PlanMonthly billingAnnual (per mo)What's included

Pro$49$44.10Per team member. 10 AI resolutions/user/mo
Plus$69$62.10Per team member. 20 AI resolutions/user/mo
Starter$59—Flat rate, unlimited team members, 500 responded conversations/mo. No AI Agent (no AI resolutions)

How AI usage is billed

Re:amaze AI Agent: 5 (Basic), 10 (Pro) or 20 (Plus) resolutions per user per month included, then $0.85 per resolution.

Estimated monthly cost by store size

Assumptions: AI resolves 50% of conversations; monthly billing unless noted; taxes, add-ons, SMS/voice and phone fees excluded. How we calculate.
